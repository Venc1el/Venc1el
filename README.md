## `01 / WHO AM I`

Hey, I'm **Sudarmo** (online: **Venc1el**) — a developer from **Surabaya, Indonesia 🇮🇩** who loves taking a product from the first sketch all the way to the final `git push`.

My day-to-day is **Flutter**, building mobile apps for **public-service and government workflows**: field officers, inspections, location-aware features, and forms that have to work reliably in the real world, not just in a demo. Around that, I'm a **full-stack developer** — I'm comfortable on the web side with **React + TypeScript**, **Node.js / Express**, and **Prisma**, and I regularly work with backend teams on **.NET** and **Node** APIs.

> **Mostly building. Occasionally overthinking. Always learning.**

## `04 / HOW I BUILD`

- 🧱 **Structure first.** Clean layers, clear naming, models that map predictably to APIs. Future-me should thank present-me.
- 🔍 **Details matter.** Spacing, empty states, loading states, error messages — the stuff users feel but never mention.
- 🤝 **APIs are a conversation.** I work closely with backend teams, read the contract carefully, and surface gaps early.
- 🚀 **Ship, then refine.** A working feature beats a perfect plan. Then I tidy it up.
